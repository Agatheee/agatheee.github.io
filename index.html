Introduction

About

I am a quantitative researcher working at the intersection of network theory, market microstructure and causal discovery. I hold a PhD in Financial Engineering from Stevens Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Zachary Feinstein.

I am currently a Quant Research Fellow at Uniswap Labs, studying on-chain market microstructure, trader and liquidity provider behavior on decentralized exchanges, dynamic fee design and thesis vaults. Before that, I spent two summers at Bloomberg LP working on causal discovery for financial time series and network models of credit risk.
